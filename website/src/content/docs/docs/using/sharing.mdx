Pantry is built for a household. Everything belongs to a **house**, and everyone in that house shares
its lists, boards and notes. On top of that, you can narrow access to individual items when you need
to.

## Houses and members

A **house** is the shared space for everyone you live with. Being a member of a house is what lets
you see and work on its checklists, Photo Board and Notes Wall. Adding and removing members is done
by a house administrator.

<Aside type="note">
	What each member can do is decided by their **roles**. Out of the box everyone can do everything;
	administrators tighten this per role. See [Permissions](/docs/administration/permissions) for the
	full model.
</Aside>

## Restricting a list to roles

By default every list is open to every member of the house. A list can instead be **restricted to
specific roles**, so only members holding one of those roles (and administrators) can open it —
useful for a list only some of the house should see. Restricting a list to roles is an
administrator action.

For a checklist, both controls live in its **Edit list** dialog: **Access** restricts the list to
roles, and **Shared with** shares it with specific people.

![The Edit checklist dialog showing the Access control that restricts a list to roles and the Shared with field that shares it with specific people.](/docs/sharing-access.png)

## Sharing a single item with people

Independently of house membership, an individual **checklist, note, photo or photo folder** can be
shared with specific people at one of two levels:

- **View** — read-only access.
- **Edit** — can change the contents (for example, check off and edit items) but not delete the whole
  thing.

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
	<TabItem label="Web">
		<Steps>

		1. Open the item — for a checklist, open **Edit list**; for a note or photo, open its actions.

		2. Under **Shared with**, add a person.

		3. Choose their access — **View** or **Edit**.

		</Steps>
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Mobile & desktop">
		<Steps>

		1. Open the checklist, note or photo you want to share.

		2. Choose **Share** from its actions.

		3. Pick the person and choose **View** or **Edit**.

		</Steps>
	</TabItem>
</Tabs>

A share can grant access to someone who would otherwise have none, but it is bounded: it never
allows deleting the shared item itself, and only the item's creator or a house administrator can
manage its shares.

<Aside type="tip">
	Use a **view** share to let someone follow a list without changing it, and an **edit** share when
	you want them to shop from it or keep it up to date.
</Aside>
