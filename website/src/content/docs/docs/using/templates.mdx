A template holds items you add again and again — your weekly staples, a packing list, everything a
guest room needs. A template is never checked off or shopped from. Its items are copied into
[checklists](/docs/using/checklists) and [inventories](/docs/using/inventories) whenever you need them: suggested as you type
a name, added one at a time, or applied all at once.

![A template called Weekly staples, with its items grouped under Bakery, Dairy and Produce, each carrying a quantity and a store.](/docs/templates.png)

## Create a template

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
	<TabItem label="Web">
		<Steps>

		1. Open **Pantry** in Nextcloud and look at **Templates** in the left sidebar.

		2. Select **New template**.

		3. Give it a name, and optionally an icon, color and description.

		</Steps>
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Mobile & desktop">
		<Steps>

		1. Open the **Checklists** tab and tap the list name at the top to open the list switcher.

		2. Pick **Templates** at the top of the switcher, then **New template**.

		3. Give it a name, a color and an icon, and tap **Create list**.

		</Steps>

		On a wide window, templates also have their own heading in the side rail.
	</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Templates stay on the web and the mobile and desktop app; the watch shows only checklists and
inventories.

A template page works like a checklist without checkboxes. Add items with the same composer, and
edit, move, copy, archive and trash them the same way. Templates have their own archive and trash.

### What a template item holds

A template item carries the same details as a checklist item — quantity, price, category, stores,
labels, custom fields, description, image and barcode — except a repeat schedule. It can also say how
it is stocked when it lands on an inventory:

- **Stock** — **Count** or **Level**, with a **Target** and a **Reorder point**. With no stock mode
  picked, it uses the inventory's default. The card spells out how the item starts, for example
  "Drawn into an inventory, it starts full with 12 on hand."
- **Expiry** — **None**, or **Keeps** a number of days. A template holds how long an item keeps, never
  a date: the date is set when the item lands on an inventory.

## Attach templates to lists

Attaching a template to a list makes its items show up as suggestions while you add items there.
Attach from either side:

- **From the list** — the checklist's or inventory's edit dialog has a **Templates:** field. Pick the
  templates whose items it suggests.
- **From the template** — **Edit template** has a **Suggest in:** field. Pick the checklists and
  inventories it suggests items on, or turn on **Every list** to cover every checklist and inventory
  in the house, including ones made later.

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
	<TabItem label="Web">
		Both fields are in the list's edit dialog: **Edit checklist**, **Edit inventory** or **Edit
		template**.
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Mobile & desktop">
		In the list switcher, open the list's **⋮** menu and tap **Edit checklist**, **Edit inventory**
		or **Edit template**. **Templates:**
		and **Suggest in:** need a connection, so they are left out of the form while you are offline.
	</TabItem>
</Tabs>

![The Suggest in field of Edit template, with the Every list switch off and Fridge and Groceries picked.](/docs/templates-suggest-in.png)

A list can have any number of templates, and a template can suggest on any number of lists. An
archived or trashed template keeps its attachments but suggests nothing until it is back in use.

## Suggestions as you type

While you type an item name on a checklist or inventory, a **Suggestions** panel shows the closest
matches: the list's own items first, then items from its templates. A template suggestion carries a
chip naming the template it comes from.

![The composer of the Groceries checklist with "Croissant" typed, suggesting the list's own Croissant and Croissants from the Weekly staples template.](/docs/templates-suggestions.png)

Pick a template suggestion to add it to the list. A template item the list already has by name is
never suggested over the list's own copy. Suggestions stay out of the way while you add several
items at once with **Multiple**, or while a detail tray is open.

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
	<TabItem label="Web">
		The panel opens under the **Item name** field.
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Mobile & desktop">
		The panel opens just above the add bar at the bottom, or below it when the add bar is set to the
		top. Template suggestions need a connection: offline, only the list's own items are suggested.
	</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Adding an item from a template

An item added from a template is a copy, so changing the template later leaves it as it is. It
starts unchecked, with the template item's name, description, category, quantity, stores, labels,
prices, custom fields, image and barcode. On an inventory it also takes the stock mode, target,
reorder point and shelf life, and starts full, keeping its shelf life from today.

<Aside type="tip">
	If the list already has an item by that name (ignoring capitals and extra spaces, archived items
	included), Pantry brings that item back instead of adding a second one: unarchived and, on a
	checklist, unchecked. It keeps its own details.
</Aside>

Categories, labels and custom fields that belong only to the template are matched by name on the
list, and added to it when the list does not have them yet.

### Filling in from item defaults

Details the template item leaves empty can start from the list's
[item defaults](/docs/using/checklists#item-defaults) — the list's usual store, say, or its
recurrence. Two switches decide whether they do:

- **Per list** — **Fill template items from these defaults** at the bottom of the list's **Item
  defaults** dialog. On by default.
- **Per person** — **Fill template items from item defaults**: **Follow each list**, **Always fill**
  or **Never fill**. Your choice here wins over the list's switch. On the web it is in **Personal
  settings**; in the mobile and desktop app, under **Settings** → **Interface** → **Adding items**.

![The Item defaults dialog of a checklist, with the Fill template items from these defaults switch turned on below the defaults.](/docs/templates-fill-defaults.png)

## Apply a whole template

To bring in many items at once — the whole weekly shop, the full packing list — apply the template.

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
	<TabItem label="Web">
		<Steps>

		1. Open the list's **⋯** menu and select **Apply template**. On a template, select **Apply to
		   list** instead.

		2. Pick what to apply: a template under **Attached to this list** or **Other templates**, or,
		   from a template, the checklist or inventory to apply it to.

		3. Untick anything you don't need this time, then select **Apply** — the button counts the
		   ticked items.

		</Steps>
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Mobile & desktop">
		<Steps>

		1. Open the list's **⋮** menu and tap **Apply template**. On a template, tap **Apply to list**
		   instead. Both are also in each list's menu in the list switcher.

		2. Pick a template under **Attached to this list** or **Other templates**, or, from a template,
		   a list under **Checklists** or **Inventories**.

		3. Untick anything you don't need this time, then tap **Apply** — the button counts the ticked
		   items. **Back** returns to the choice.

		</Steps>
	</TabItem>
</Tabs>

![The Apply template dialog copying from Weekly staples into Groceries, with Eggs, Bananas, Tomatoes and Croissants ticked, and Bread, Milk, Butter and Yoghurt unticked and marked Already on list.](/docs/templates-apply.png)

Items the list already has open are marked **Already on list** and start unticked. Every ticked item
is added just as a single one would be, so one the list has checked off or archived is brought
back rather than duplicated. The message afterwards says how many were added and how many brought
back.

## Duplicate as another kind

**Duplicate checklist**, **Duplicate template** and **Duplicate inventory**, in a list's menu (and,
in the mobile and desktop app, **Duplicate** in each list's menu in the list switcher), ask what to
**Duplicate as:** — **Checklist**, **Inventory** or **Template** — so one list can
become the start of another kind:

- Duplicate a template as a checklist for each trip or project, and work through the copy while the
  template stays as it is.
- Save a checklist that worked well as a template.
- Start an inventory from a template of everything a cupboard should hold.

![The Duplicate template dialog for Weekly staples, with Checklist picked under Duplicate as.](/docs/duplicate-list.png)

The copy takes the items with their details, the list's own categories, labels and custom fields,
its item defaults, and its icon, color and access. Items arrive unchecked; duplicating a checklist as
a checklist can keep them as they are with **Set all items to undone** turned off. Repeat schedules
do not carry into an inventory, and a list's attached templates carry over only when it stays a
checklist or inventory.
