The Photo Board is a shared wall of photos for the household — the receipt to keep, the shelf you
need to match, the model number to remember. Photos are stored in your own Nextcloud, and organized
into folders.

<Aside type="note">
	Photos live in your Nextcloud files, shared with the house — see
	[Permissions](/docs/administration/permissions) for who can view, upload and manage them.
</Aside>

![The Photo Board in the Pantry web app — a grid of household photos with New folder and Upload actions.](/docs/photo-board.png)

## Upload photos

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
	<TabItem label="Web">
		<Steps>

		1. Open the **Photo Board**.

		2. Select **Add image**, or drop image files straight onto the board to upload them.

		</Steps>
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Mobile & desktop">
		<Steps>

		1. Open the **Photo Board** tab.

		2. Tap the add button and pick a photo from your device or camera.

		</Steps>
	</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Captions

Open a photo to add or edit its **caption** — a short line describing what it is, so the board stays
readable when it fills up.

## Folders

Group related photos into folders — one per project, room or trip.

- **Create a folder** to start a new group.
- **Move a photo** into a folder, or between folders, to reorganize the board.
- Reorder folders and photos to put what matters first.

## Trash

Moving a photo or folder to the trash soft-deletes it. Trashed photos can be restored, or
permanently deleted, from the trash view.

<Aside type="caution">
	Permanently deleting a photo, or emptying the photos trash, cannot be undone.
</Aside>
