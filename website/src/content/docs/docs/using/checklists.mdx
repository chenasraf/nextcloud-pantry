Checklists are the heart of Pantry: shared shopping and to-do lists that everyone in the house sees
update in real time. An item can carry a quantity, a price, a category, one or more stores, labels,
an image, and a repeat schedule.

<Aside type="note">
	The web interface and the [companion app](/docs/getting-started/pairing) work on the same
	lists in your Nextcloud. Anything you change in one shows up in the other.
</Aside>

![A checklist in the Pantry web app, with items grouped under category headers and carrying quantities, prices, stores, labels and a weekly repeat.](/docs/checklists.png)

Each list has settings for its name, description, icon, color and access, plus a **default
recurrence** that decides what new items on it start as:

- **Remember last** — a new item copies the recurrence of the last item added to this list.
- **None** — new items stay on the list after they are marked done.
- **One-time** — new items are removed from the list once marked done.
- **Recurring** — new items come back on the schedule you set alongside.

**All lists** shows every item across the house in one place.

## Add and check off items

A composer adds an item to the list. Checking an item marks it done: it strikes through and drops
to the bottom, and can be un-checked to bring it back.

The composer carries a row of chips — **Category, Stores, Labels, Quantity, Price, Description,
Recurrence, Image, Barcode** — so you can set an item's details as you add it.

![The checklist composer with chips for category, stores, labels, quantity, price, description, recurrence, image and barcode.](/docs/checklists-composer.png)

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
	<TabItem label="Web">
		- The composer sits at the **top** of the list, in a card titled **Add item**. Type a name in
		  the **Item name** field, set any chips you want, and press **Enter** or **Add**.
		- Click a row's checkbox to mark it done or undone.
		- Clicking elsewhere on a row does whatever your **Default item click action** setting says —
		  out of the box, nothing.
		- On a touch screen, long-press a row to start selecting several at once.
		- Each row's menu holds view, edit, copy to list, move to list, archive and trash.

	<TabItem label="Mobile & desktop">
		- The composer is pinned to the **bottom**, labeled **Add to …** with the list's name (or
		  **Add an item …** on All lists). Type there and tap send to add an item.
		- Tap a row's checkbox to mark it done or undone.
		- Tapping elsewhere on a row opens the item by default, and long-pressing starts a
		  multi-select — both are configurable.
		- Swipe a row for quick actions — view, edit, move, copy, archive, delete.
	</TabItem>

<Aside type="tip">
	Checked items never just vanish — they strike through and stay recoverable, so an accidental tap is
	always undoable.
</Aside>

What a tap or click on a row does is up to you, in **Settings**:

### Adding something the list already has

As you type a name, matching items already on the list appear under the composer as **Already on
this list**. Tap one to reuse it instead of creating a second row for the same thing — bringing a
checked item back rather than duplicating it.

Two settings shape this:

- **Reuse existing items** — what happens when the name you add already exists: **Always ask** (the
  default — a prompt offering **Reuse existing** or **Add anyway**), **Always reuse**, or **Never
  reuse**.
- **Suggest archived items** — off by default; turn it on to search archived items for suggestions
  too. Reusing an archived item unarchives it.

## Item details

Set any of these from the composer chips as you add an item, or open an existing item to change them:

- **Quantity** — free text, so a unit comes along for the ride ("3", "400 g", "2 x 1 L"). The **+**
  and **−** buttons step the number inside it and leave the rest alone, in a 1-5-10 progression that
  grows with the value — 8 → 9 → 10 → 15, 400 → 450 → 500 — so a large quantity is a few taps away
  rather than a hundred.
- **Price** — an amount (or a range) used to estimate shopping totals.
- **Category** — one category, shown as a colored header that groups the list.
- **Stores** — one or more shops where the item is bought.
- **Labels** — free-form tags for filtering.
- **Image** — attach a photo so everyone recognizes the exact product.
- **[Custom fields](/docs/using/custom-fields)** — your own fields (a text note, a number, a
  yes/no flag, a date, or a list of choices) that you define for the house or a single list.

![A checklist item showing its details inline — quantity, price, an attached image, a weekly repeat and its store.](/docs/checklists-item.png)

### Barcodes

Scan or enter a product **barcode** (EAN or UPC) to prefill a new item from it — Pantry looks up the
product and fills in its name, category and photo for you to review before adding. If no product
matches, fill in the item yourself.

You can **scan** with your camera on the phone app, and in the web interface when a camera is
available; on the desktop app, type the barcode in by hand.

## Organize with categories, stores and labels

Three independent ways to organize a list:

- **Categories** group items under colored headers (Bakery, Dairy, …). An item has at most one.
- **Stores** mark where each item is bought (Supermarket, Home Depot, …), and drive
  [Shopping Mode](/docs/using/shopping-mode) routes.
- **Labels** are free-form tags for anything else.

You create and manage each set from its picker — **Create category…**, **Create store…**,
**Create label…** — and can rename, recolor or delete them there.

Above the list, a free-text filter plus **Categories**, **Labels**, **Stores** and **Price** filters
narrow what you see.

![The filter row above a checklist — a text filter and dropdowns for categories, labels, stores and price.](/docs/checklists-filters.png)

## Recurrence

Every item is one of three kinds, set from the **Recurrence** chip:

- **Staple** — stays on the list after it is marked done.
- **One-time** — removed from the list once marked done.
- **Recurring** — comes back on a schedule.

A new item starts as whatever its list's [default recurrence](#create-a-list) says.

### Repeat schedules

A recurring item repeats on a schedule — for the groceries you buy every week. Presets cover
**Daily**, **Weekly**, **Every 2 weeks** and **Monthly**; past those, build the rule yourself from
**Every _n_** days, weeks, months or years:

- **Weeks** — the weekdays to repeat on.
- **Months** — either **days of the month** (leave it empty to repeat on the same day each month),
  or **a weekday of the month**: First, Second, Third, Fourth or Last, paired with a weekday — the
  second Tuesday, the last Friday.
- **Years** — a **date of the year** (leave it empty to repeat on the same date each year).

A repeat can also **end**: never, after a number of occurrences, or on a date. A summary of the rule
you built is shown as you go.

Pantry brings the item back on its **next recurrence** after it is checked off, anchored one of two
ways:

- **On a fixed schedule** (default) — the item comes back on its next scheduled date, no matter when
  you actually tick it off. A weekly item stays on the same weekday even if you shop a few days late.
- **Starts after completion** — turn this on to count the interval from when the item is ticked off
  instead. A "every week" item you buy two days late then returns a full week _from that purchase_,
  not from the original date.

## Import and export

You can move lists in and out of Pantry as Markdown checklists:

- **Import from Markdown** — paste a Markdown list or drop a `.md` file to create items in bulk.
- **Export to Markdown** — copy the list out as a Markdown checklist.

You can also **copy** or **move** items to another list from the item menu.

## Archive and trash

- **Archive** an item to take it off the active list without deleting it — archived items stay
  available to bring back later. Whole lists can be archived too.
- **Trash** an item or list to soft-delete it. Trashed things can be restored, or permanently
  deleted, from the trash view.

Once a shop or a chore is behind you, the **Done** section's menu clears it in one go:

- **Archive all** moves every checked item in the list to the archive.
- **Remove all** moves every checked item in the list to the trash.

Both cover the whole list rather than only what a filter or search currently shows. Each asks for
confirmation first, and the message that follows carries an **Undo**.

<Aside type="caution">
	Emptying the trash or permanently deleting cannot be undone. Everything else — checking off,
	archiving, trashing — is recoverable.
</Aside>
