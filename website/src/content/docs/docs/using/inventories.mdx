An inventory tracks what you have at home — the pantry shelf, the fridge, the cleaning cupboard —
and how much of each thing is left. When something runs low or starts to go off, Pantry puts it on
your shopping list. When you tick it off there, the inventory is restocked.

![The Pantry inventory, grouped under Bakery, Dairy and Dry goods: Bread at Full, Butter out, Eggs in stock with a date, Milk expiring tomorrow, Flour low, Jam in stock and Rice not counted.](/docs/inventories.png)

## Create an inventory

	<TabItem label="Web">
		<Steps>

		1. Open **Pantry** in Nextcloud and look at **Inventories** in the left sidebar.

		2. Select **New inventory**.

		3. Give it a name, and optionally an icon, color and description.

		</Steps>
	</TabItem>

Add items with the same composer as a checklist, with two extra chips: **Stock** and **Expiry**.
Items are organized, edited, archived and trashed just as on a [checklist](/docs/using/checklists).
Inventories have no checkboxes: you change how much is left instead.

## Counting stock

Each item is tracked one of two ways, picked under **Stock** in the item dialog:

- **Count** — for things you can count. **On hand** is how many you have, **Target** how many you
  want ("12 eggs"), and **Reorder point** the amount at which it counts as low. Without a reorder
  point, anything below the target is low. At 0 it is out.
- **Level** — for things you'd rather not count, like flour or spices: **Full**, **Low** or **Out**,
  plus a **Restock amount** — what you buy when it runs low ("1 kg bag").

Stock amounts are free text like any quantity, so units come along: "4 eggs", "2 L". A new count
item starts full, with its target on hand, unless you give it an amount. When on hand has no number
("some"), the item is **Not counted**: it is shown but never runs low.

Switching an item between Count and Level converts its values, and the dialog shows what it
converted them to before you save.

![The Stock and Expiry cards of the item dialog for Eggs: Count, with 4 eggs on hand, a target of 12 eggs and a reorder point of 3, and an expiry that keeps 21 days.](/docs/inventories-stock-expiry.png)

### Reading a row

- The **status icon** in front of each item is green when it is in stock, amber when it is low or
  expiring, red when it is out or expired, and a grey question mark when it is not counted.
- **Chips** under the name say what needs doing: **Low · buy 8 eggs**, **Out · buy 4 blocks**,
  **Expires tomorrow · buy 2 L**, **Expired Oct 21 · buy 1 carton**. The amount is what goes on the
  shopping list. An item in date shows **Good until** its date.
- A **gauge** under the chips fills with what is on hand, with a tick at the reorder point. A level
  item shows three segments for Full, Low and Out.
- **− and +** on a count item step the number and keep the words around it ("4 eggs" → "5 eggs").
  A level item has a **Full / Low / Out** control instead.

Items that need restocking stay under their category: a status is only ever a filter.

### Finding what needs restocking

- In the sidebar, each inventory shows how many of its items need restocking.
- The **Stock** filter above the list shows **All items**, only those that **Need restock** (low,
  out, expiring or expired), or those **In stock**.

## Expiry dates

Under **Expiry** in the item dialog, choose how an item expires:

- **None** — it does not expire.
- **Date** — the date it is good through. A date after today also sets how long it keeps.
- **Keeps N days** — how long it keeps once stocked. The date becomes today plus that many days,
  and every restock renews it.

An item is **expiring** from a few days before its date, and **expired** from the day after.
**Expiry warning**, in the inventory's settings, sets how many days ahead to warn — 2 by default, or
0 to warn on the date itself. An item that keeps only a short time is not flagged straight after it
is restocked. An item with nothing left on hand is never flagged as expiring: there is nothing to
go off.

Expiry never changes your stock: throwing food away is your call. An expiring item asks the shopping
list for a full replacement — its target, or its restock amount.

## From the inventory to the shopping list

### The restock checklist

Pick a **Restock checklist** in **Inventory settings**. Items that run low or start expiring are
added to it, or put back on it when they are already there. Without one, they are only marked on the
inventory, and the inventory says so above the list.

![The Inventory settings dialog for Pantry, with Groceries as the restock checklist, an expiry warning of 2 days and the daily stock summary turned on.](/docs/inventories-settings.png)

### Linked items

Each inventory item is linked to the one checklist item that restocks it:

- **Automatically** — the first time it is needed, Pantry looks on the restock checklist for an
  item with the same name (ignoring capitals and extra spaces), and adds one when there is none.
  When several items match, nothing is linked until you pick one.
- **By hand** — link it to any item on any checklist you can edit, for example to keep a separate
  row per inventory.

A linked item keeps its link even when either side is renamed. Several inventories can share one
checklist item: Pantry and Fridge milk both restock **Milk** on Groceries, which then asks for what
they both need.

On a wide screen, the link button on each row shows the link and opens it. The item dialog's
**Shopping list** card does the same at every width.

![The Shopping list card of the item dialog for Butter: linked to Butter on Groceries, which asks for 4 blocks, with Unlink and Link to a different item buttons.](/docs/inventories-shopping-list.png)

![The link popover on the Butter row: Butter on Groceries with an Unlink button, and a Link by hand search listing checklist items.](/docs/inventories-link.png)

**Unlink** turns off **Add to shopping list when low**, so the item is only marked on the inventory.
Turn the switch back on to have it linked again when it is next needed.

### What the shopping list asks for

- A low **count** item asks for what it is missing to reach its target.
- A low **level** item asks for its restock amount.
- An **expiring** or **expired** item asks for a full replacement.
- A checked-off or archived item is put back on the list; one in the trash is replaced by a new one
  on the restock checklist.
- A message tells you whenever your change added, brought back or updated an item on the list.

### Amounts set by hand

Change the quantity of a linked item on the shopping list and stock leaves it alone: it reads
**set by hand** until the item is next checked off. Tap the **✕** on the chip to let stock set it
again.

![A Milk item on the Groceries checklist, with the restocks marker before its name and a "× 4 L · set by hand" chip.](/docs/inventories-pinned.png)

### No longer needed

When stock recovers before anyone shops — someone stepped it back up, or renewed the date — the
item on the shopping list is checked off for you and reads **No longer needed**. An item someone
reopened by hand stays on the list until it is checked off.

## Buying restocks the inventory

Checking off a linked item — on the checklist or in [Shopping Mode](/docs/using/shopping-mode) —
puts what you bought back into stock:

- The **first number** in the item's quantity is what you bought. Left as stock asked, that is
  exactly what was missing; set by hand, it is what you actually bought. Without a number,
  everything it restocks goes back to full.
- A **level** item goes back to **Full**.
- Items **expiring or expired** are replaced rather than added to: the new stock takes the old
  stock's place.
- Every item it restocks gets a **fresh date** — today plus how long it keeps, or no date when it
  has no shelf life.
- An item shared by several inventories fills them in the order they were linked; anything left over
  goes to the first. One that came up short goes straight back on the list for the rest.

The checked-off item says what it did until it is unchecked:

![The checked-off Butter item on Groceries, reading "Restocked Butter (Pantry) +4 · uncheck to undo".](/docs/inventories-restocked.png)

<Aside type="tip">
	Ticked the wrong thing? **Uncheck it** to take the stock back out and put the old date back. A
	date someone changed by hand since stays as it is. **Uncheck all** and a repeat schedule
	reopening an item leave stock alone.
</Aside>

### Raising stock by hand

Stepping an item up from empty — or moving a level item up from **Out** — counts as new stock and
renews its date. Stepping up stock that is already there may only be a recount, so the date stays,
and the row offers **Renew expiry →** the renewed date instead. Tap it if you did buy more; any other change
to the item, or leaving the page, puts the offer away.

## Daily stock summary

Once a day, at the house's **Recurring items reopen time** (set in
[Manage house](/docs/administration/manage-house)), Pantry sends one notification per inventory
about what newly ran low or out, or started expiring or expired since the last one — for example
"Pantry: 1 low, 1 out, 1 expiring". Opening it shows the inventory's items that need restocking.
Nothing new, no notification.

Everyone who can see the inventory gets it. Turn it off for the whole inventory with **Daily stock
summary** in **Inventory settings**, or just for yourself under **Personal settings** →
**Notifications**.
