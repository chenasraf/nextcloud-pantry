Everything in Pantry belongs to a **house** (see [Permissions](/docs/administration/permissions) for
how a house scopes access). The **Manage house** page is where you set that house's own options —
its name, the times its background jobs run, how long the trash is kept, and who belongs to it.

Open it from the house menu in the sidebar.

<Aside type="note">
	Only house **admins** can open Manage house. The **Danger zone** below is narrower still — it
	shows only to the house **owner**.
</Aside>

Most fields save together from the **Save changes** button in the toolbar; leaving with unsaved
edits prompts you first. Members, roles, shopping reminders, and deleting the house each have their
own flow.

## General

- **Name** — what the house is called throughout Pantry. Required.
- **Description** — an optional short line of context for the house.

## Automation

Both times run in the **server timezone**, and a background job acts on them every 15 minutes — so
an item comes due within about a quarter-hour of the time you set, not to the exact second.

- **Recurring items reopen time** — the time of day a [recurring item](/docs/using/checklists#repeat-items)
  becomes due again once its schedule comes around. Defaults to 08:00.
- **Custom field reminder time** — the time of day that date
  [custom-field reminders](/docs/using/custom-fields) are sent. Defaults to 08:00.

## Trash

- **Days to keep items in the trash** — a daily background job permanently deletes checklists,
  items, notes and photos whose deletion is older than this many days. Set it to **0** to never
  auto-delete — trashed things then stay until removed by hand. Defaults to 30, and accepts a whole
  number up to 3650.

## Shopping

- **Manage reminders** — opens the reminders that pop up during [Shopping Mode](/docs/using/shopping-mode)
  — at the start of a trip, when changing store, and at the end. This section appears only to
  members who can edit lists.

## Members & roles

- **Manage members** — add or remove people and assign each one a role.
- **Manage roles** — define what each role is allowed to do.

Both open their own dialogs; the full role and capability model is covered in
[Permissions](/docs/administration/permissions).

## Danger zone

- **Delete house** — permanently removes the house and all of its lists, items, and membership
  records. Pantry asks you to confirm first.

<Aside type="caution">
	Deleting a house cannot be undone. Everything inside it goes with it.
</Aside>
