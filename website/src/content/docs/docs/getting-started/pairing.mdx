The app on your phone or desktop talks to the same Pantry app running on your Nextcloud. Pairing a
client means pointing it at your server and signing in with your Nextcloud account — the app then
sees the same houses, lists, boards and notes as the web interface.

<Aside type="note">
	Install the [server app](/docs/getting-started/install) first. The apps do nothing on their own — they
	are a view onto your Nextcloud.
</Aside>

## What you need

- A Nextcloud server with the Pantry app enabled.
- Your Nextcloud account on that server.
- The Pantry app installed — see [Get the mobile & desktop app](/docs/getting-started/apps).

## Connect

When you open the app for the first time it asks for your server. Enter your **Server URL** — just
the address, like `cloud.example.com`; the app adds `https://` for you.

Then sign in one of two ways.

<Tabs>
	<TabItem label="Browser login (recommended)">
		<Steps>

		1. Enter your **Server URL** and tap **Connect**.

		2. Your Nextcloud login page opens. Sign in there and **Grant access** to the app.

		3. Return to the app — it finishes on its own once you have approved access.

		</Steps>

		This uses Nextcloud's standard login flow, so the app never sees your password. It receives a
		device-scoped token instead.
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="App password">
		If the browser login will not open — or your server uses a self-signed certificate — sign in
		with a Nextcloud app password instead.

		<Steps>

		1. In Nextcloud, go to **Settings → Security → Devices & sessions** and create a new app
		   password.

		2. In the app, tap **Sign in with an app password instead**.

		3. Enter your **Server URL**, **Username** and the **App password**, then tap **Sign in**.

		</Steps>
	</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Both methods register a device under **Settings → Security → Devices & sessions** in Nextcloud,
which you can revoke at any time.

<Aside type="tip">
	If your server uses a self-signed certificate, the app shows the certificate's SHA-256 fingerprint
	and asks you to confirm it before trusting it. Check the fingerprint matches the one your server
	administrator gave you, then trust it to continue.
</Aside>

## Multiple houses

If your account belongs to more than one house, the app lets you switch between them. Everything you
do stays scoped to the house you have open, exactly as it is on the web.
