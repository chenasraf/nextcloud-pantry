## Highlights

### Items

- Adding items is quicker. Type "2 L milk" and Pantry fills in the amount and suggests a category,
  based on what your household picked for that item before. Each guess shows as a chip you can drop,
  and anything you type or pick yourself always wins. It works when adding one item, several at
  once, or importing a list. Turn it off with "Smart input" in Settings.

### Shopping

- Finished a shopping trip by mistake? Open it in your shopping history and choose "Undo trip": the
  items go back on their lists and what the trip put away comes back out of your inventories.
  Anything someone changed since is left alone, and Pantry tells you which items those were.

## New

### Items

- Pantry remembers the category and amount you pick for each item, across the whole household, so
  its suggestions get better over time. The mobile app uses the same memory.
- Items sold at several stores no longer crowd their row with store chips. One chip shows how many
  stores carry the item, and clicking it lists them.

### Lists

- Checklists add up the prices of the items you still need to buy. Filter or search the list and the
  estimate follows along, so you can see what one project or one store run will cost before you head
  out. (web)

### Notes

- A note saved to a file in someone else's files shows whose files it is in, like
  `@alice/Notes/Plants.md`. When the file is in a folder shared with you, the note shows where it is
  in your own files instead. (web)

### Photos

- Add pictures you already keep in your Nextcloud files to the photo board or to an item, without
  uploading them again. Link a picture to show it right where it is, or import a copy into Pantry.
  Everyone in the household can see it, and removing it from Pantry never deletes your original
  file.

### Templates

- Templates can now fill themselves in. Turn on "Automatically add new items from attached lists" in
  a template's settings, and anything you add on the lists it's attached to is saved to the template
  too, ready to suggest on your other lists. Items a template already has stay exactly as you set
  them up.

### Web

- After an update, Pantry shows you what's new once, the first time you open it. Close it on one
  device and it stays closed on the others. (web)

## Fixes

### Lists

  ones from their lists.

### Translations
