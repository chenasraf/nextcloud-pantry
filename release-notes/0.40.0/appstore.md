Adding items is quicker. Type "2 L milk" and Pantry fills in the amount and suggests a category,
based on what your household picked for that item before. Each guess shows as a chip you can drop,
and anything you type or pick yourself always wins. It works when adding one item, several at once,
or importing a list, and you can turn it off with "Smart input" in Settings.

Finished a shopping trip by mistake? Open it in your shopping history and choose "Undo trip": the
items go back on their lists and what the trip put away comes back out of your inventories. Anything
someone changed since is left alone, and Pantry tells you which items those were.

- Pantry remembers the category and amount you pick for each item, across the whole household, so
  its suggestions get better over time, on the web and in the mobile app.
- Add pictures you already keep in your Nextcloud files to the photo board or to an item, without
  uploading them again: link the file where it is, or import a copy.
- Checklists add up the prices of the items you still need to buy, and the estimate follows your
  filters and search.
- Templates can add new items from the lists they are attached to on their own. Turn it on in the
  template's settings; items a template already has stay as you set them up.
- After an update, Pantry shows you what's new once. Close it on one device and it stays closed on
  the others.

Items sold at several stores show a single chip you can click to see them all. Setting a category or
labels on several items at once offers the ones from their lists. A note saved in someone else's
files shows whose files it is in. Updated translations.
